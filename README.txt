BEFORE SUBMISSION:
1. Open index.html and replace [Your Full Name] with your full name.
2. Replace [Your Registration Number] with your actual registration number.
3. Update the sample email and phone number in the Contact section with your preferred contact details.
4. Upload the project folder to Netlify or GitHub Pages and submit the live website URL.

Note: The website uses online image and font links, so an internet connection is needed for those images and fonts to appear.
